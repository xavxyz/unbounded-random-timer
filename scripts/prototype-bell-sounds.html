What is this?

Sounds for a calm phone timer for Taiji / gong fu practice. Practice alternates two phases, White and Black. A sound marks the start of each, at hidden, random moments, with the practitioner's eyes often closed. So each sound must be noticed without startling, and the two must be told apart by ear alone. The shortest gap between two sounds is 5 s; the longest is 15 min.

Below are four starting points (switch with the yellow bar). Ring them, shape them with the sliders, and try Start session to hear them as they'd arrive in practice. Real bells, bowls and gongs are welcome as references: anything is up for change.

When you like one, press Copy params on White and on Black and send both back, or Download .wav to try them elsewhere.
