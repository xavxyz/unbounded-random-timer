# Unbounded Random Timer

A calm phone timer for Taiji / gong fu training. It rings at hidden, random intervals within bounds you set, and shows only the round number.

## Language

### The session

**Session**:
One practice, from the tap that begins it to the hold that stops it.

**Round**:
One pass through every enabled phase, in the chosen order. Rounds are numbered from 1, with no upper limit.
_Avoid_: Cycle, set

**Phase**:
A stretch of a round whose duration is drawn at random within its bounds. There are two, White and Black, each named after its background colour; the app gives them no other meaning.
_Avoid_: Timer, track

**White**:
The phase shown in black on white. Practitioners typically use it for action.
_Avoid_: Action

**Black**:
The phase shown in white on black. Practitioners typically use it for integration.
_Avoid_: Integration, rest, pause

**Bounds**:
The shortest and longest duration a phase can draw, set to the second.
_Avoid_: Range, limits

**Hold to stop**:
Holding anywhere on the screen until the session stops. As the hold goes on, the round number fades into the background colour; a haptic fires as the stop lands.
_Avoid_: Press to stop, reset
